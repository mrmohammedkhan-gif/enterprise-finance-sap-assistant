Invoice Approval Policy

1. Invoices up to £10,000 may be approved by a Finance Manager.

2. Invoices above £10,000 require approval from the Finance Director.

3. Any overdue invoice must be reviewed before approval.

4. Invoices from inactive vendors must be rejected.

5. Approval recommendations must not be treated as final payment authorisation.

6. All approval decisions must be recorded in the finance audit log.
